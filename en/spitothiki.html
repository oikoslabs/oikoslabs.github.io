Spitothiki: your household stock, shopping and expenses in one app

A free app for your household stock, shopping and expenses. It works without an account. No ads, and we don't sell data.

Open Spitothiki

Note: the app itself is in Greek.

The app

Sample screens with test data, no account needed. The app interface is in Greek.

What Spitothiki does

  • Scan the QR code on an AADE receipt (the Greek tax authority) and the items and total are filled in for you.
  • See what is about to expire in your stock.
  • Keep a shopping list and share it with your household, if you sign in.
  • A household calendar with expiry dates, bills and events.
  • A monthly budget.
  • Export receipts to PDF.
  • Recipes.
  • Fuel and trip logging.
