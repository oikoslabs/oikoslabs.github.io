Privacy

Privacy policy

The full privacy policy for Spitothiki is on the app's own page: spitothiki.web.app/privacy.html (in Greek).

According to that policy:

  • Spitothiki is a free app for your household stock, shopping and expenses.
  • It has no ads, we don't sell data and we don't use tracking cookies.
  • Stock, lists, expenses and settings are stored on your device. If you don't sign in, they never leave it. The app works without an account.
  • If you sign in (Google or email), your data is synced to Firebase and only the members of your household can see it.
  • We use anonymous, cookie-free analytics (Cloudflare Web Analytics) and anonymous measurement of where visits come from, with no personal data.

MonLogistics WMS

  • The demo runs only in your browser with test data and does not send data anywhere.
  • The full app stores warehouse data in Firebase (Google), and only users with an account created by the administrator can see it.
  • Only your sign-in and changes that have not been sent yet are kept on the device.

For questions or to request account deletion, write to oikoslabs.apps@gmail.com.
